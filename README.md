# Colchester North District — Expense Claim Form

An online expense claim form for Colchester North District Scouts. Volunteers can log event expenses and mileage throughout the month, then submit to auto-generate emails to their manager and district finance (James Chandler).

## Features

- Event expenses with categories (equipment, catering, venue, first aid, etc.)
- Mileage tracking at 45p/mile (HMRC approved rate)
- Receipt file upload (up to 5 files)
- **Auto-saves to the browser** — entries persist across sessions on the same device
- Auto-generates three emails on submission: manager, finance, and claimant confirmation
- Unique claim reference number per submission (CND-YYYY-MMDD-XXX)
- Finance email pre-set to james.chandler@cnds.org.uk

## How to deploy on GitHub Pages

### Step 1 — Create a GitHub account
If you don't have one, go to [github.com](https://github.com) and sign up for free.

### Step 2 — Create a new repository
1. Click the **+** button (top right) → **New repository**
2. Name it something like `expenses` or `expense-form`
3. Set it to **Public**
4. Click **Create repository**

### Step 3 — Upload the file
1. On your new repository page, click **Add file** → **Upload files**
2. Drag and drop `index.html` into the upload area
3. Scroll down and click **Commit changes**

### Step 4 — Enable GitHub Pages
1. Go to your repository **Settings** (tab at the top)
2. Scroll down to **Pages** in the left sidebar
3. Under **Branch**, select `main` and click **Save**
4. After a minute or two, your form will be live at:
   `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/YOUR-REPO-NAME/`

### Step 5 — Share the link
Send the URL to all section leaders in Colchester North District. They can bookmark it and use it every month.

## Making updates

If you need to change anything (e.g. update the finance email, add a category):

1. Open the repository on GitHub
2. Click `index.html`
3. Click the **pencil icon** (Edit)
4. Make your changes
5. Click **Commit changes**

The live site updates automatically within a minute.

---

## How volunteers use it

1. Open the bookmarked link
2. Fill in their details (saved automatically — they can come back later)
3. Add expense items and/or mileage journeys throughout the month
4. At month end, upload receipts and click **Submit claim & send emails**
5. Three email drafts open — they review and send each one
6. The form clears ready for next month

> **Note on receipts:** Receipt files cannot be saved between sessions — volunteers will need to re-attach them at submission time. Advise leaders to keep receipt photos in a phone album or folder as they go.
